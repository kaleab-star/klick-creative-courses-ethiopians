# Best Film School in Ethiopia 2027: How to Choose (Addis Ababa)

Disclosure: this page is published by Klick Film & Design School, one of the schools in Addis Ababa. It is our own view, not a neutral ranking. Visit several schools before you pay.

## What to check in any film school in Ethiopia

1. Hands-on work on real projects from the first week, not only slides.
2. The software and gear you actually use: Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, cameras and lights.
3. Class size and whether you get your own computer time.
4. Total fee and length of the course, and whether you can pay level by level.
5. Intakes, schedule and whether weekend or online classes exist.
6. Student work you can watch before you enrol.
